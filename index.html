The Origin

Built from the seat
of a pickup truck.

"I got tired of doing the math in my head at a fuel stop, accepting loads that looked good on paper, and realizing 400 miles later I barely broke even."

I'm Bryan — hotshot trucker, owner-operator, and founder of The AI Trucker. I built LoadCalc for myself first. Every feature exists because I needed it on the road: voice input because I can't type while driving, overnight detection because lodging kills margins, load profiles because my MPG changes dramatically based on what I'm hauling.

The AI Trucker is the beginning of something bigger — an AI-powered operations platform built for independent contractors and small businesses. But it all started with one question: is this load worth it?
